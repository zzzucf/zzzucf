# Zihang Zou

I study machine learning with a focus on data ownership, image watermarking, and the safety and reliability of neural models. My research includes detecting unauthorized use of personal images in training and evaluating image copyright protections against diffusion-model transformations.

[Google Scholar — publications and citations](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GLuGAK0AAAAJ&hl=en) · [GitHub research repositories](https://github.com/zzzucf?tab=repositories)

## Selected research and code

### Neural Plagiarism · ICCV 2025

**Attention to Neural Plagiarism: Diffusion Models Can Plagiarize Your Copyrighted Images!**  
Zihang Zou, Boqing Gong, and Liqiang Wang

Studies the robustness of visible and invisible image copyright markers under diffusion-model transformations, using a training-free anchor-and-shim approach.

[Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Zou_Attention_to_Neural_Plagiarism_Diffusion_Models_Can_Plagiarize_Your_Copyrighted_ICCV_2025_paper.html) · [Code](https://github.com/zzzucf/Neural-Plagiarism) · [BibTeX](https://github.com/zzzucf/Neural-Plagiarism/blob/main/citation.bib)

### Anti-Neuron Watermarking · ECCV 2022

**Anti-Neuron Watermarking: Protecting Personal Data Against Unauthorized Neural Networks**  
Zihang Zou, Boqing Gong, and Liqiang Wang

Studies personal data ownership verification by watermarking images with linear color transformations and inferring the resulting signature from a trained neural classifier.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09023) · [Published version](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-19778-9_26) · [Code](https://github.com/zzzucf/anti-neuron-watermarking) · [BibTeX](https://github.com/zzzucf/anti-neuron-watermarking/blob/main/citation.bib)
